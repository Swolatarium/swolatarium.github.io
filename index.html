THE SWOLATARIUM

Fitness is for everyone!

Do you think strength training isn’t for you? Do you feel uncomfortable in a regular gym? The Swolatarium is just the place for you. I work with plenty of “unconventional” gym goers from geeks and nerds to LGBTQ+ folks to a range of health conditions.

The Swolatarium provides personal training, structured training, program design, small group training and physical therapy services.

I have been working as a trainer since 2011 and I train general fitness clients as well as competitive athletes.

I am also a Doctor of Physical Therapy allowing me to tailor your strength training program to your body, including any injuries you may have or have had, including injury and surgical recovery, and return to play/sports training.
